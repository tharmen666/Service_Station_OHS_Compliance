# Demo Script: Service Station OHS Compliance (3 minutes)

**Live link:** https://service-station-ohs-compliance.vercel.app
**Format:** screen recording with on-screen subtitles. Voice-over can be added later over the same timing.
**Target length:** about 3:00. Each scene shows its start time and the subtitle to display.

---

### Scene 1: What this is (0:00–0:20)
**Screen:** home page, header visible with the site code.
**Subtitle:** "Forecourt OHS compliance for service stations. AI drafts. A competent person decides."
**Voice (optional):** "This is a compliance tool for service station forecourts. It helps teams draft statutory documents. It does not replace the competent person who signs them."

### Scene 2: Operational modules (0:20–0:55)
**Screen:** click "Fuel Spill Containment", then "Employee Rights", then "Training".
**Subtitle:** "Spill response, employee rights and statutory training, in one place."
**Voice (optional):** "Spill response steps, employee rights under the OHS Act, and statutory training courses with expiry tracking, all in one place."

### Scene 3: Wetstock and fuel accounting (0:55–1:15)
**Screen:** click "Wetstock & Offload", then "Wetstock ATG".
**Subtitle:** "Daily reconciliation and offload checks, with variance tracking."
**Voice (optional):** "Daily stock reconciliation and delivery checks, so losses and gains are visible early."

### Scene 4: Honest AI settings (1:15–1:45)
**Screen:** open "AI Studio", show "Temperature Requested: 0.0" and the line "AI-drafted output requires human review". Close the drawer.
**Subtitle:** "Temperature 0.0 is requested for repeatability. It does not guarantee accuracy."
**Voice (optional):** "The AI is configured to be repeatable, but repeatable is not the same as correct. The panel says so plainly."

### Scene 5: Draft a statutory document (1:45–2:25)
**Screen:** open "Autonomous Statutory OHS Doc Generator". Type a site name and a hazard description. Click Generate. Scroll to show the DRAFT notice and the citations section.
**Subtitle:** "Every document starts as a draft. Personal details are removed before anything leaves the server."
**Voice (optional):** "We enter a site and a hazard. The server removes personal details such as ID numbers and phone numbers. The draft is marked DRAFT and lists what must be checked."

### Scene 6: Reviewer sign-off gate (2:25–2:50)
**Screen:** show the export button greyed out. Type a reviewer name, tick the confirmation. The export button becomes active.
**Subtitle:** "Export is locked until a named competent person reviews and confirms the draft."
**Voice (optional):** "Export stays locked until a named competent person confirms they have reviewed the draft. The export records who signed off and when."

### Scene 7: Close (2:50–3:00)
**Screen:** back to the home page.
**Subtitle:** "Governance first: draft only, named reviewer, audit record. Questions welcome."
**Voice (optional):** "Governance first. Drafts only, a named reviewer, and an audit record for every document. Thank you."

---

## Production notes
- Keep the hazard text in the recording generic, with no real names or ID numbers.
- The generator calls the live model. If the model is unavailable, the template draft is used, and the page still shows the DRAFT notice.
- Before recording, confirm the live link responds and the header shows the site code.
- Do not claim accuracy, compliance or approval in narration. Use the words in the subtitles.

# DRAFT Operator Agreement Template (POPIA Act 4 of 2013, sections 20–21)

> **DRAFT. NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** This template lists the clauses a responsible party and an operator usually need to cover. A South African commercial lawyer and a POPIA specialist must draft, review and approve the final agreement before any personal information is processed.

**Parties**
- Responsible party (the station owner or franchisee): [legal name, registration number, Information Officer name]
- Operator (the service provider): [legal name, registration number, Information Officer name]

**1. Roles.** The responsible party decides why and how personal information is processed. The operator processes it only on the responsible party's written instructions (s20–21).

**2. Purpose and scope.** The operator processes employee and contractor personal information only to generate OHS statutory drafts and maintain training and audit records for the sites listed in Annex A. Categories: names, roles, training records, incident notes. Special or sensitive categories: [state any, e.g. health information]. Nothing else.

**3. Instructions.** Processing outside Annex A requires written instructions from the responsible party.

**4. Security safeguards (s19).** The operator maintains the measures in Annex B, including access control, encryption in transit (TLS), redaction of identity numbers and contact details before any third-party processing, audit logging, and staff confidentiality.

**5. Sub-processors and AI model providers.** The operator lists every sub-processor in Annex C, including the hosting provider and any AI model provider, with its country of processing. Each sub-processor is bound to terms no less protective than this agreement.

**6. Cross-border transfers (s72).** Personal information leaves South Africa only if one of the s72 conditions applies (for example, a binding agreement or adequate protection). The parties record the condition relied on for each transfer in Annex C. [Lawyer to confirm.]

**7. Breach notification (s22).** The operator notifies the responsible party without undue delay after any suspected compromise, with the facts known at that time, so the responsible party can notify the Information Regulator and affected people.

**8. Data subject requests.** The operator forwards access, correction and deletion requests to the responsible party within [2] business days and assists with the response.

**9. Retention and deletion.** Records are kept for [period set by the responsible party, consistent with OHS and COIDA record-keeping duties]. On termination, the operator returns or deletes the information and confirms this in writing, subject to any legal duty to retain.

**10. Audit.** The responsible party may audit the operator's compliance with this agreement once a year and after any breach, on reasonable notice.

**11. Liability and indemnity.** [Lawyer to draft.] The operator does not replace the responsible party's OHS duties. Statutory drafts are reviewed and signed off by a competent person named by the responsible party.

**12. Term and termination.** [Lawyer to draft.]

**13. Governing law.** The Republic of South Africa.

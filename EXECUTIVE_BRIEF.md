# EXECUTIVE BRIEF: AUTONOMOUS OHS & ERGONOMIC GOVERNANCE
**Platform:** ErgoSafe Autonomous Compliance Architecture  
**Target Focus:** Statutory Risk Mitigation, Operational Continuity & Workforce Resilience  

---

### Executive Summary
Modern enterprise operations face strict statutory enforcement under the **Occupational Health and Safety Act (Act 85 of 1993)**, the **2019 Ergonomics Regulations**, and **POPIA**. Traditional safety management relies on fragmented paper files, delayed incident reporting, and unverified manual inspections.

**ErgoSafe** replaces passive administration with an autonomous, real-time governance engine. By combining deterministic statutory threshold monitoring, cryptographic export validation, and zero-install edge workflows, ErgoSafe converts OHS compliance from an overhead expense into an automated shield against operational liability.
